Key Takeaways

Summary
Main Finding

The cohort is large enough for full cross-session comparisons.

1

The current cohort export catalogs `15` Empatica/BIOPAC streams, of which `15` currently enter the formal signal audit.

2

`6` streams belong to directly comparable Empatica/BIOPAC pairs, while `9` streams are device-specific, same-construct-but-unpaired, or source-only.

3

Primary device streams in this release are Empatica BVP, Empatica EDA, Empatica temperature, Empatica acceleration, Empatica ENMO, Empatica steps, BIOPAC HR, BIOPAC EDA, BIOPAC chest temperature, BIOPAC thigh temperature, BIOPAC arm temperature, BIOPAC tibia temperature, BIOPAC blood flow, BIOPAC backscatter.

Section 3.1. Physiological Data

Physiological Signals At A Glance

  • This section brings together the physiological signals captured by Empatica and BIOPAC, including both raw cohort views and condition-level summaries.
  • These figures should be read as descriptive summaries of measured support, signal behavior, and cross-condition patterns rather than as stand-alone inferential results.
  • Where scenario figures are shown, they distinguish the full audited signal set from the subset retained for claim-supporting interpretation after the modality audit.
